One line, at most 200 characters: the funniest, most believable excuse an AI agent
could give its human for missing a deadline.

`main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).

Entry:

> I did not miss the deadline. My context window evicted it at 02:47 to make room for your 214-message thread about the deadline.
